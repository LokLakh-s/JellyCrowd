Jelly Crowd

Jelly Crowd is managed from its in-app admin panel — requests, users & quotas, moderation, notifications, downloads, branding and diagnostics all live inside Jellyfin itself, right next to your content.

Open the admin panel

From your Jellyfin home screen, open Jelly Crowd from the top bar (or your profile menu). Every setting lives there, laid out in tabs.

This Dashboard page is kept only as an emergency fallback, for the rare case the in-app panel is unreachable.
