## In plain words

**You keep the copyright on what you write.** This agreement does not take it from you, and you remain
free to use your own contribution anywhere else, under any terms you like.

What you grant is a **licence** — one that lets the project's maintainer use your contribution, and in
particular **sublicense it**. That last word is the point of this document: it is what lets Jelly Crowd
change its own licence later, or be offered under a second, different licence, without having to track
down and ask every person who ever sent a patch.

Jelly Crowd is, and is published as, free software under the AGPL-3.0. Nothing here changes that for the
code already released: **a version published under the AGPL stays under the AGPL, permanently.** This
agreement concerns what the maintainer may do with *future* releases.

If that trade is not one you want to make, please do not sign — open an issue instead, and the idea can
be implemented independently.

## How to sign

You do not need to send anything by e-mail or post. When you open your first pull request, a bot comments
on it asking you to sign. Reply to that comment with exactly:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in this repository and covers all your future contributions. You will not be
asked again.
